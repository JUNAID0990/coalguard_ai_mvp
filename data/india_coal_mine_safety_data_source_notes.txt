Synthetic mine-level feature dataset grounded in the supplied Coal Directory of India 2024-25 PDF (28-11-2025a-stc.pdf).
Source-grounded counts: 373 operational coal mines and 20 operational lignite mines as of 31-03-2025; state allocations reproduced from the PDF tables.
Mine-level safety, inspection, violation, action, incident, environmental, zone, and contractor fields are simulated because the source PDF provides mostly aggregated statistics and does not provide all requested fields per mine.
Do not interpret simulated values as official mine-specific measurements.
Rows: 393; columns: 47 including mine_id and state.
